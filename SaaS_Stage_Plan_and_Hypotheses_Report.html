How to run the hypothesis tests

  • 1–2: Interviews + landing page with pricing by region.
  • 3–4: Same landing + one outbound or content push; tag traffic/signups by location.
  • 5–6: MVP with Stripe (SGD/GBP/USD); track signup → payment by country.
  • 7: Survey or interview after first 5–10 customers per region.
  • 8: Simple A/B test (e.g. headline or subhead) by region.
  • 9: Funnel dashboard with segment = country/region.
  • 10: Retention table: cohort = signup month, segment = region.
